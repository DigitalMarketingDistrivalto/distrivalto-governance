02 Why Now

Growth has outpaced governance.

Distrivalto operates three brands across many countries on more than a dozen platforms. That scale is an asset — until ownership, access, and measurement fall behind it.

01

Multi-brand complexity

Holstein Housewares, Connecto, and Alessa each run independent digital footprints with distinct audiences and funnels.

02

US and LATAM expansion

Active growth in the US and LATAM regions like: Ecuador, Peru, Colombia, Mexico, P.R., D.R., Bolivia and others, demands consistent structure, not market-by-market improvisation.

03

Platform sprawl

Over seventeen platforms and tools in active use — each a potential point of access risk, data loss, or measurement gap.

04

Revenue accountability

A business of this scale deserves a marketing function measured in CAC, ROAS, and LTV — not impressions alone.

03 Current State

Where the risk lives today.

A candid assessment — not of effort, but of structure. These gaps are common at this stage of growth, and each is directly fixable.

Access

Fragmented platform ownership

Admin rights scattered across different individual accounts — with no single source of truth.

Tracking

Incomplete measurement layer

GA4, GTM, and conversion tracking not fully validated across all brand properties.

Process

No unified governance model

Access and approval decisions happen case by case, with no defined chain of ownership.

Reporting

Inconsistent performance visibility

No standing dashboard connecting spend, leads, and revenue across brands and markets.

04 The Solution

A governance-first operating system.

Before optimization comes structure. This framework establishes clear ownership, complete visibility, and a repeatable process for every platform, every brand, every market.

Audit first

Full inventory of every asset and access point before any change is made.

Centralized access

One governed structure for administration, replacing scattered ownership.

Measurable by design

Tracking validated at the source, so every dashboard can be trusted.

Built to scale

A playbook designed to extend cleanly into new brands and markets.

05 Governance Model

One chain of ownership.

Every platform, every brand — the same clear hierarchy of accountability, from strategic ownership to daily execution.

06 Platforms

The full digital surface.

Every platform under one governance standard — each with a defined owner, access policy, and audit trail.
